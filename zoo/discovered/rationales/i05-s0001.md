Idea:
The primary goal of this evolution is to deepen the 'efficient spiking neural network nature' of the `SpikingFLABlock` by removing redundant continuous activation functions. In `[Version 2]`, both `SpikingMLP` and `SpikingResidualGate` employed `F.gelu` after a `SpikingLinear` layer. The `SpikingLinear` module itself already incorporates a spiking neuron that converts a continuous input into a binary spike, and the subsequent `nn.Linear` layer operates on these binary spikes, producing a continuous output. Applying an additional `F.gelu` after this continuous output (derived from binary spikes) introduces a continuous non-linearity that diminishes the network's reliance on the intrinsic non-linearity of the spiking mechanism.

**Implemented Modifications:**
1.  **Removed `F.gelu` from `SpikingMLP`**: The line `x = F.gelu(x)` in `SpikingMLP.forward` has been deleted. This means the non-linearity in the MLP is now solely derived from the spiking neurons within the `SpikingLinear` layers.
2.  **Removed `F.gelu` from `SpikingResidualGate`**: The line `x_act = F.gelu(x_cont)` in `SpikingResidualGate.forward` has been deleted. This similarly ensures that the residual gating mechanism relies on the spiking neuron's non-linearity.

**Motivation and Expected Improvements:**
*   **Enhanced SNN Purity**: By removing `F.gelu`, the architecture more faithfully embodies the principles of Spiking Neural Networks. The non-linearity is primarily a consequence of the neuron's thresholding and firing, rather than a separate continuous function. This should lead to a clearer signal processing path consistent with neuromorphic computing paradigms.
*   **Reduced Computational Overhead**: Eliminating the `GELU` operation, while seemingly minor, contributes to a simpler and potentially more efficient network by reducing the number of complex floating-point operations. This aligns with the objective of creating 'efficient spiking neural network nature'.
*   **Clearer Attribution of Non-linearity**: This change forces the model to learn and exploit the non-linear properties inherent in the spiking neurons more effectively, potentially leading to more interpretable and biologically plausible learning dynamics within the SNN context.
*   **Potential for Improved Generalization**: By simplifying the non-linearity and making it more specific to spiking dynamics, the model might generalize better by avoiding over-reliance on complex continuous transformations that might not always be optimal for spike-based computation.

All other constraints, such as preserving `SpikingLinear`, maintaining function signatures, using `einops.rearrange`, ensuring batch-size independence, and collecting `final_spikes` correctly, have been strictly adhered to. The core `mamba2_ssd_parallel_stable` operator and the `SpikingResidualGate` mechanism (minus the `GELU`) from `[Version 2]` are retained due to their demonstrated performance benefits and innovative contributions.
