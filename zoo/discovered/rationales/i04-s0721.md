Idea:
The primary architectural enhancement in this version focuses on re-integrating and refining spike-driven beta modulation within the `DeltaNet` attention mechanism. This change addresses the 'neuromorphic computing natures' objective by introducing a direct feedback loop where the network's internal spiking activity dynamically influences the scaling factor (`beta`) in the attention-like computation.

**Key Architectural Innovations and Their Implementation:**

1.  **Re-introduction of Spike-driven Beta Modulation in `DeltaNet`:**
    *   **Implementation:** The `spike_beta_mod_proj` layer (a `SpikingLinear` module) is re-added to `DeltaNet`'s `__init__`. This layer is designed to project a scalar mean spike activity into a multi-head modulation signal.
    *   **Mechanism:** In the `DeltaNet`'s `forward` pass, `k_spike_mean_activity` is computed by taking the mean of the binary spikes from the `k_proj` layer across the feature dimension. This mean activity is then fed into `self.spike_beta_mod_proj`. The analog output of this `SpikingLinear` (along with its own binary spikes) is used to modulate the original `beta` factor via a `tanh` activation, ensuring a bounded and soft modulation.
    *   **Rationale:** This directly connects the dynamic spiking behavior of the network (specifically the key projections) to a critical parameter (`beta`) that controls the scaling of the value vector `v` and key-beta product `k_beta` in the `delta_rule_chunkwise` function. This allows the network to adapt its attention mechanism based on its own firing patterns, promoting a more biologically plausible and adaptive information processing.
