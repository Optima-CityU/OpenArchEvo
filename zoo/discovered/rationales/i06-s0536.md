Idea:
The primary modification in this evolutionary step focuses on enhancing the adaptivity of the `SpikingResidualGate` module. Recognizing that the modulation strength of the residual connections plays a critical role in how the model integrates information and adapts to varying levels of neural activity, a learnable `range_multiplier` parameter has been introduced.Previously, the modulation factor in `SpikingResidualGate` was bounded by a fixed range, specifically `1.0 + 2.0 * sigmoid(...)`. While effective, this fixed range limited the model's capacity to dynamically adjust the influence of spike-activity-driven modulation. By making the `2.0` multiplier learnable as `self.range_multiplier`, the model can now autonomously determine the optimal scaling for its residual gates. This allows for a more flexible and powerful adaptive mechanism, enabling the network to learn stronger or weaker modulation effects based on the task and the current spiking dynamics.This change adheres to the principles of neuromorphic computing by introducing a more adaptive, activity-dependent control mechanism without adding significant computational complexity. It extends the "Spike-Activity Modulated Residual" concept by allowing the network to learn the *degree* of modulation itself, fostering more robust and efficient information processing in a spiking context. This modification represents a targeted performance engineering solution, allowing the network to more effectively leverage its internal spiking dynamics for improved cognitive potential and adaptive computational mechanisms, directly building upon the empirically superior Version 2 architecture.
