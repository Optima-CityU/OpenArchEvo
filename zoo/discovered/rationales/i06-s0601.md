Idea:
This architectural evolution introduces a novel spike-driven beta modulation mechanism within the `DeltaNet` attention block. 

**Motivation for the Change:**
1.  **Enhanced Neuromorphic Integration:** In spiking neural networks, information is primarily conveyed through sparse, binary spike events. The original `DeltaNet` computed the `beta` parameter (which modulates `key` and `value` vectors in the Delta Rule) based on the *analog* `hidden_states` input, even though it passed through a `SpikingLinear` layer. By changing the input of the `self.b_proj` (beta projection) to the *binary spikes* generated by the `q_proj` (`spikes_q_proj`), we ensure that the modulation of the attention mechanism is directly driven by the actual spike activity in the query stream. This more deeply embeds the spiking nature into the core attention dynamics.
2.  **Adaptive Spike-driven Saliency:** The `beta` parameter plays a critical role in scaling the `key` and `value` contributions. By making `beta` adaptive to the sparsity and activity patterns of the query spikes, the network can dynamically adjust its focus. If a query stream exhibits sparse spiking, indicating less immediate salient information, the `beta` value might also be driven towards lower values, leading to a more localized or subdued influence of corresponding keys and values.
