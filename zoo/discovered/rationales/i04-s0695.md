Idea:
The primary goal of this evolution was to refine the internal processing within the `SpikingFLABlock` to further enhance its "efficient spiking neural network nature" while maintaining a robust SNN-ANN hybrid architecture. Building upon the performance improvements observed in Version 2 (which simplified the `SpikingMLP` by removing non-spiking activations and dropouts), these changes aim to streamline analog signal paths and reduce redundant or overly complex non-spiking operations.

1.  **Refined `qk_activation` logic in `DeltaNet`**: The previous `DeltaNet` implementation contained a potential redundancy where `F.silu(q)` and `F.silu(k)` might be applied twice if `ShortConvolution` was active and its internal activation was also `silu`. This modification ensures that the `qk_activation` (e.g., `silu`, `relu`, `elu`) is applied only once, either by the `ShortConvolution` layers (if `use_short_conv` is true and `qk_activation` is `silu`) or directly to `q` and `k` when `ShortConvolution` is not used. This eliminates unnecessary redundant non-linearities and promotes a cleaner, more efficient analog signal flow. This aligns with the general principle of simplification that led to performance gains in Version 2.

2.  **Simplified `beta` modulation in `DeltaNet`**: The original `beta` modulation mechanism, which used `beta_spike_mod_analog.tanh()`, introduced a relatively complex non-linear function (`tanh`) on an analog signal derived from spike activity. While `SpikingLinear` layers intrinsically handle the spike-to-analog conversion, excessive subsequent analog non-linearities can detract from the "spiking-native" efficiency. This change replaces `tanh` with a simpler `sigmoid` function, followed by a scaling factor (`* 2`). `sigmoid` is a common and computationally lighter choice for gating or modulation, offering a bounded output that directly scales the main `beta` value. This modification ensures a more direct and computationally plausible influence of spike activity on the `beta` parameter, contributing to the "efficient spiking neural network nature" by simplifying analog processing after spike integration.
