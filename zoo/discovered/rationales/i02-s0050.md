Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to ensure complete adherence to the spiking neural network paradigm throughout the `SpikingFLABlock` and to enhance code clarity and correctness. 

**1. Enforcing Uniform Spiking Nature (Removal of `use_spiking_mlp` and `MLP` class):**
The original `SpikingFLABlock` allowed for a non-spiking `MLP` via the `use_spiking_mlp` flag. This created a hybrid architecture that sometimes deviated from the core objective of developing efficient spiking neural networks. By removing the `use_spiking_mlp` flag and consequently the non-spiking `MLP` class, the `SpikingFLABlock` now exclusively utilizes `SpikingMLP`. This guarantees that all feed-forward operations within the block pass through spiking neurons (embedded within `SpikingLinear` layers), fully aligning with the requirement to replace all `nn.Linear()` layers with `SpikingLinear()` and ensuring that the inputs to all linear layers are binary signals produced by spike neurons. This simplifies the architecture and makes its SNN nature explicit and consistent.

**2. Correcting Spike Collection in `SpikingFLABlock.forward`:**
A critical bug was identified in the `SpikingFLABlock.forward` method's spike collection logic. The variable `mlp_spikes` was initialized to `None` but never updated, leading to a flawed conditional check (`if mlp_spikes is not None:`). This prevented the spikes generated by `SpikingMLP` (i.e., `mlp_spikes1` and `mlp_spikes2`) from being correctly added to the `all_spikes_list`. The fix involves directly checking and appending `mlp_spikes1` and `mlp_spikes2` after the `SpikingMLP` forward pass, ensuring that all emitted spikes are accurately collected and returned as `final_spikes`.

**3. Removing Redundant Neuron in `SpikingMLP`:**
The `SpikingMLP` class previously included `self.neuron = neuron_registry[neuron_type](...)` in its `__init__`, but this `self.neuron` instance was never actually used in its `forward` method. The `SpikingLinear` wrapper already encapsulates the spike neuron functionality directly before its internal `nn.Linear` layer. Removing this redundant neuron simplifies the `SpikingMLP`'s implementation, making it more concise and eliminating unnecessary instantiation, while maintaining the intended spiking behavior through its `SpikingLinear` components.

**4. Adherence to `einops.rearrange` and Batch-Size Agnosticism:**
All operations continue to use `einops.rearrange` for dimension manipulation, maintaining robustness to varying batch sizes and avoiding hardcoded dimensions, as required by the technical specifications.

These modifications collectively lead to a more coherent, robust, and explicitly spiking neural network block, improving its theoretical grounding in neuromorphic computing principles and ensuring correct functionality for spike signal collection.
