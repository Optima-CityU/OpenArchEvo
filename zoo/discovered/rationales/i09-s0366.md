Idea:
The primary architectural innovation in this version of the `SpikingFLABlock` is the enhancement of the residual gating mechanism through a `ContextualSpikeActivityGate`. The previous `SimpleSpikeActivityGate` relied on a single averaged spike activity. This new design allows for a more sophisticated, context-aware modulation of residual connections by aggregating multiple relevant spike signals from within each block.

**Motivation for the `ContextualSpikeActivityGate`:**
1.  **Enriched Contextual Gating:** Instead of a single, global average spike activity per block, the `ContextualSpikeActivityGate` takes a concatenation of mean spike activities from *specific*, important internal projections (e.g., `q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, and the final `o_proj` for the attention block; `fc1` and `fc2` for the MLP block). This provides the gating mechanism with a richer, more nuanced understanding of the block's dynamic firing patterns.
2.  **Adaptive Information Flow:** By learning to combine these distinct spike statistics, the gate can dynamically adjust the contribution of the residual path based on the specific "computational state" indicated by the diverse spiking behaviors within the block. For instance, high activity in `k_proj` vs. `q_proj` might signal different contextual relevance, which the gate can now leverage.
3.  **Neuromorphic Plausibility:** Neurons in biological brains integrate signals from multiple sources before firing. This multi-input gating mimics this principle, allowing a more complex, spike-driven decision process for information routing.
4.  **Preservation of SNN Principles:** The `ContextualSpikeActivityGate` itself adheres to the strict SNN requirements. Its internal `nn.Linear` layer receives binary signals produced by a spiking neuron (via `SpikingLinear`). The input to this gate is also derived from the mean activities of binary spikes, maintaining the flow of spike-derived information.

**Key Implementation Details:**
-   **`ContextualSpikeActivityGate` Class:** A new `nn.Module` is introduced that takes `num_spike_features` as an argument, allowing it to aggregate different numbers of mean spike activities. It uses `SpikingLinear(nn.Linear(num_spike_features, 1, bias=False))` to project these aggregated features to a single modulation factor.
-   **Spike Feature Selection:** For the attention block, mean spike activities from `q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, and the final `o_proj` of the `SpikingGatedDeltaProduct` are collected. For the MLP block, mean spike activities from `fc1` and `fc2` of the `SpikingMLP` are used.
-   **Robust Aggregation:** The forward pass of `SpikingFLABlock` now explicitly collects these individual mean spike activities, concatenates them, and feeds the resulting `(B, L, num_spike_features)` tensor to the respective `ContextualSpikeActivityGate`.
-   **Handling Empty Spikes:** Robust checks are added to handle cases where spike tensors might be `None` or empty, ensuring that zero tensors of the correct shape are used as placeholders to maintain dimensional consistency for concatenation.

This refined gating mechanism provides a powerful, yet compliant, way to introduce more adaptive and context-sensitive control over information flow within the `SpikingFLABlock`, contributing to potentially superior cognitive capabilities and model performance in sequence modeling tasks.
