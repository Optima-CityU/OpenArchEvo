**Motivation for Spike Activity Regularization:**
Spike activity regularization is a cornerstone of training efficient Spiking Neural Networks. Its re-introduction serves several key purposes, deeply aligned with neuromorphic computing principles:
1.  **Energy Efficiency & Sparsity**: Biological neural networks are remarkably energy-efficient, largely due to sparse and event-driven communication (spikes). High firing rates are metabolically expensive.
