Idea:
The primary motivation for this evolution is to enhance the model's capacity for Extended Context Memory and Multi-Scale Information Integration, while strictly adhering to the Spiking Neural Network (SNN) nature and computational efficiency constraints. Version 2 introduced adaptive gain and input/output gating, which was a good step, but its residual connection was still a simple additive one after the output gate, without an explicit mechanism to 'forget' previous information in the residual pathway.

This new design, 'Dual-Gated Residuals with Temporal Context,' reintroduces a GRU-like dual-gating mechanism (forget and add gates) for the residual connections, both after the SpikingKDA attention block and the SpikingMLP. This is crucial for enabling the model to: 

1.  **Selectively Integrate New Information**: The 'add' gate allows the model to adaptively control how much of the current block's output (attention or MLP) is integrated into the residual path.
2.  **Selectively Forget Old Information**: The 'forget' gate provides a mechanism to modulate or diminish the influence of previous contextual information carried by the residual path. This explicit forgetting capability is vital for managing long-range dependencies and preventing information accumulation or saturation, directly addressing 'Extended Context Memory' improvements.
3.  **Contextual Adaptation**: The gating factors are not static; they are dynamically generated by feeding a combination of the original residual pathway and the current branch's output through dedicated `SpikingLinear` layers. This ensures the gates adapt to the current input context, supporting 'Adaptive Computational Mechanisms'.
4.  **Multi-Scale Temporal Context**: To further enrich the gating decisions, `ShortConvolution` layers are reintroduced *before* the dual-gating projections. These convolutions provide local temporal context to the combined residual and branch signals, allowing the gates to make decisions based on short-range temporal patterns, thereby facilitating 'Multi-Scale Information Integration' and aligning with 'locality and statefulness of computation'.

**Implementation Details:**

*   **Elimination of Redundancy**: The previous `attn_input_gain_proj`, `attn_input_gate_proj`, `attn_output_gain_proj`, `attn_output_gate_proj` (and their MLP counterparts) from Version 2 have been removed. Their functionalities are now subsumed and enhanced by the more sophisticated dual-gated residual structure, leading to a cleaner architecture.
*   **SpikingLinear Compliance**: All new linear projections introduced for the dual-gating mechanism (`attn_residual_forget_proj`, `attn_residual_add_proj`, `mlp_residual_forget_proj`, `mlp_residual_add_proj`) are implemented using `SpikingLinear`. This ensures that their internal `nn.Linear` layers receive binary spikes, as mandated. The inputs to these `SpikingLinear` layers are continuous values (from `ShortConvolution` or direct addition of continuous features), which is consistent with the `SpikingLinear` class's design where it handles the conversion to spikes internally.
*   **ShortConvolution for Gating Context**: Two new `ShortConvolution` layers (`attn_gating_context_conv` and `mlp_gating_context_conv`) are added to process the combined residual and branch output before feeding it into the dual-gating `SpikingLinear` layers. This integrates local temporal information directly into the gating decision process.
*   **Cache Management**: The caching for the newly added `ShortConvolution` layers is integrated into the `past_key_values` mechanism, ensuring statefulness across timesteps in inference mode.
*   **Spike Collection**: All spikes generated by the new `SpikingLinear` layers are meticulously collected and concatenated into the `final_spikes` output, maintaining the required output format.

This refined architecture represents a significant step towards more sophisticated and adaptively controlled information flow within an SNN, leveraging established principles of recurrent gating and temporal context to improve sequential modeling capabilities.
