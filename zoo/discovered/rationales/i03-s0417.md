Idea:
The evolution from Version 1 to Version 2 highlighted the efficacy of the `LightNet` attention mechanism and a simpler `SpikingMLP` structure (removing `GELU`). Building upon this, the current version introduces a 'deeply spiking' enhancement to the `SpikingMLP` to further enforce neuromorphic principles and improve information flow consistency within the feed-forward block.

**Implemented Architectural Modifications:**
1.  **Intermediate Spiking Activation in MLP**: A new explicit spiking neuron (`self.intermediate_spike_activation`) has been inserted into the `SpikingMLP` between the first and second `SpikingLinear` layers. Instead of the analog output of the first linear layer (`fc1.linear`) directly feeding into dropout and then `fc2` (which then internally spikes its input), the analog output of `fc1.linear` now first passes through this dedicated intermediate spiking neuron.
    *   **Old flow (Version 2 `SpikingMLP`)**: `Analog_in -> SpikingLinear1(neuron1->linear1) -> Analog_out_1 -> Dropout -> Analog_out_after_dropout -> SpikingLinear2(neuron2->linear2) -> Analog_out_2`
    *   **New flow (Deep Spiking MLP)**: `Analog_in -> SpikingLinear1(neuron1->linear1) -> Analog_out_1 -> IntermediateSpikingNeuron -> Binary_Spikes_Intermediate -> Dropout -> Binary_Spikes_After_Dropout -> SpikingLinear2(neuron2->linear2) -> Analog_out_2`

2.  **Dropout on Spikes**: The dropout layer within `SpikingMLP` now operates directly on the binary spikes produced by the new `self.intermediate_spike_activation`. This design choice aligns with research suggesting that dropout can be applied to spike trains, acting as a form of sparse connection pruning or noise injection, which is compatible with SNNs and can enhance robustness.

3.  **Comprehensive Spike Collection**: All three distinct sets of binary spikes generated within the `SpikingMLP` (from `fc1.neuron`, `self.intermediate_spike_activation`, and `fc2.neuron`) are now explicitly collected and concatenated into the `final_spikes` tensor, providing a more complete representation of the network's spiking activity.

**Theoretical Grounding and Expected Improvements:**
*   **Enhanced Neuromorphic Consistency**: By introducing an explicit intermediate spiking activation, the MLP transitions from a hybrid analog-spike processing to a more consistently spike-driven computation internally. This adheres more closely to biological principles where information within a neural pathway is primarily encoded and transmitted via spikes.
*   **Improved Sparsity and Efficiency**: Forcing an intermediate binary spike representation can promote greater sparsity in activations, potentially leading to more energy-efficient computations (as non-firing neurons contribute zero) and more robust representations. The dropout applied directly to spikes further encourages sparsity and resilience.
*   **Clearer Information Pathway**: The explicit spike-to-spike transitions within the MLP provide a clearer, more constrained information pathway, which can lead to better learning dynamics and interpretability, especially in long sequence modeling tasks where signal integrity is crucial.
*   **Gating and Locality**: The `intermediate_spike_activation` acts as a form of non-linear gating, deciding which signals propagate based on a threshold. This introduces a form of inherent locality and statefulness at the neuron level, complementing `LightNet`'s recurrent state-based mechanism.

**Compliance with Requirements:**
*   All `nn.Linear` layers are wrapped by `SpikingLinear`.
*   The `SpikingLinear` class remains unmodified.
*   `final_spikes` is a flattened `torch.Tensor` of binary spikes.
*   Sub-quadratic complexity is maintained, as the `LightNet` attention is preserved, and the MLP modifications are linear in `d_ffn`.
*   `einops.rearrange` is used, and batch size independence is ensured.
*   The `SpikingFLABlock` class name and `forward` signature are preserved.
*   `**kwargs` in `__init__` is maintained. No `@torch.compile` or `bf.float16`.
*   Padding in `lightnet_chunkwise` now uses `value=0.` and `dtype=q.dtype` for the initial state for robustness.
