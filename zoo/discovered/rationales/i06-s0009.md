### Key Architectural Innovations

1.  **Spike-Gated Residual Connection (SGRC)**:
    *   **Implementation**: Two new `SpikingLinear` layers, `self.attn_output_gate_proj` and `self.mlp_output_gate_proj`, have been introduced within the `SpikingFLABlock`. These layers take the analog output of the attention (`DeltaNet`) and MLP (`SpikingMLP`) sub-blocks, respectively. Each `SpikingLinear` generates its own internal spikes and produces an analog output (which is then passed through a sigmoid activation to form a gate). This gate then element-wise multiplies the corresponding sub-block's output before being added to the residual pathway.
    *   **Neuromorphic Computing Natures**: This modification profoundly enhances the neuromorphic characteristics of the block. Instead of traditional additive residual connections that simply combine analog signals, the SGRC introduces a dynamic, spike-driven modulation of the information flow. The gate's value is directly influenced by the spiking activity originating from the sub-block's output, making the residual path's contribution context-aware and regulated by the neuron's firing dynamics. This mirrors biological mechanisms where neuronal activity can gate the impact of signals.
    *   **Adaptive Computational Mechanisms**: The SGRC enables adaptive information processing. Sub-blocks that generate specific spiking patterns or higher firing rates will produce different gating signals, allowing the model to selectively emphasize or suppress certain features. This provides a mechanism for dynamic resource allocation and signal prioritization based on the incoming data's characteristics as interpreted by the spiking neurons.
    *   **Efficiency**: The added `SpikingLinear` layers and subsequent element-wise multiplication operations maintain linear computational complexity, adhering to the sub-quadratic efficiency requirement. They integrate seamlessly without introducing significant overhead.

2.  **Batch Size Independence Fix in `DeltaNet`**:
    *   **Implementation**: The problematic lines responsible for collapsing the batch dimension (i.e., `get_unpad_data`, `index_first_axis`, and `pad_input` calls that manipulated `hidden_states` in `DeltaNet`'s `forward` method) have been removed. The `DeltaNet` now expects and operates on `hidden_states` of shape `(B, L, D)`, where `B` is any batch size and `L` is the (potentially padded) sequence length. `delta_rule_chunkwise` handles its own internal padding to `chunk_size` multiples.
    *   **Environmental Robustness**: The previous implementation implicitly restricted the effective batch size to 1 for the core attention mechanism when an `attention_mask` was provided, which violated the critical requirement for batch-size agnostic operation. This fix ensures that the `SpikingFLABlock` (and `DeltaNet` within it) can robustly process inputs with *any* batch size, leading to universal compatibility and consistent performance across different training and inference configurations.

3.  **Uniform Spiking Layer Usage & Spike Output Format**:
    *   **Implementation**: All new gating mechanisms leverage the `SpikingLinear` class, ensuring that every linear transformation in the evolved architecture is preceded by a spiking neuron. The `final_spikes` tensor accurately collects binary spike outputs from all `SpikingLinear` layers, including the new SGRC gates, by flattening and concatenating them as `torch.cat([s.flatten() for s in all_spikes_list])`.
    *   **Core Objective Alignment**: This strictly adheres to the core objectives of maximizing the spiking neural network nature and correctly returning a raw binary spike tensor, rather than a firing rate or a list of tensors, thus meeting the specified output format.
