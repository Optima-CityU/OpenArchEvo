Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to streamline and enhance the spike activity regularization mechanism within the SpikingFLABlock, promoting a more cohesive and efficient spiking neural network (SNN) design. Historically, in previous versions, spike regularization was distributed, with DeltaNet calculating its own internal regularization and SpikingFLABlock then adding regularization for MLP spikes. This distributed approach could lead to redundant calculations, potential inconsistencies in applying the regularization weight across different sub-components, and a less clear global optimization objective for spike sparsity.

**Key Architectural Innovations Implemented:**

1.  **Centralized Spike Regularization:** The spike activity regularization for the entire `SpikingFLABlock` is now centralized. Instead of `DeltaNet` calculating its own `current_reg_loss` and returning it, `DeltaNet` (and `SpikingMLP`) now *only* return the binary spike tensors generated by their internal `SpikingLinear` layers. The `SpikingFLABlock` aggregates *all* these binary spikes (from both the attention mechanism and the MLP) into a single flattened `final_spikes` tensor. It then calculates the mean firing rate of these aggregated spikes and applies the `self.spike_reg_weight` to this single, comprehensive spike activity metric. This ensures a unified and consistent regularization objective across the entire block.

2.  **Simplified `DeltaNet` Interface:** To support the centralized regularization, the `DeltaNet.forward` method's return signature has been simplified. It now returns only the analog output (`o`) and the dictionary of collected binary spikes (`all_spikes`), no longer an internal regularization loss. This reduces coupling between `DeltaNet` and `SpikingFLABlock` regarding regularization, making `DeltaNet` a more focused and reusable sub-component.

3.  **Default `spike_reg_weight` in `SpikingFLABlock`:** The `spike_reg_weight` is now explicitly part of the `SpikingFLABlock.__init__` signature with a sensible default, ensuring clear parameterization and consistency.

**Research Insights and Expected Improvements:**

*   **Neuromorphic Efficiency:** Centralizing spike regularization promotes a more efficient SNN nature. By calculating a single, block-wide regularization loss, the network is encouraged to find a globally optimal sparse spiking activity, which is a hallmark of energy-efficient neuromorphic systems. This can lead to lower overall firing rates, reducing computational load during inference.
*   **Improved Optimization Landscape:** A unified regularization objective provides a clearer gradient signal for the optimizer to encourage sparse spiking across the entire block. This can lead to a more stable and effective training process, potentially improving model convergence and generalization (as observed in improved performance of Version 2 over Version 1, which now has this principle refined).
*   **Clean Structure and Low Coupling:** Decoupling the regularization calculation from `DeltaNet` enhances the modularity of the architecture. `DeltaNet` becomes a pure attention mechanism that exposes its spiking behavior, while `SpikingFLABlock` orchestrates the overall block-level spike dynamics and regularization.
