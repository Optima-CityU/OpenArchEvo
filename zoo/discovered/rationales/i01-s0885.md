Idea:
The improved SpikingFLABlock introduces two key architectural innovations to enhance adaptive computational mechanisms, multi-scale information integration, and the overall spiking neural network (SNN) nature, while maintaining sub-quadratic complexity and efficient chunk-wise processing.

### SpikingHGRN_Adaptive Enhancements:
1.  **Contextual Decay Modulator:** A new 'contextual decay modulator' is introduced in `SpikingHGRN_Adaptive`. This modulator provides an additional, fine-grained control over the recurrence decay rate (`g` in HGRN2). While the existing `decay_modulator` provides a global head-wise adjustment, the `contextual_decay_modulator` offers an independent, adaptive signal derived from the input, further enriching the dynamic behavior of the recurrent states. This allows the model to adapt its memory retention characteristics based on immediate contextual cues, addressing 'Extended Context Memory' and 'Adaptive Computational Mechanisms'. Both `decay_modulator` and `contextual_decay_modulator` are multiplied with `(1. + modulator_value)` to the log-sigmoid decay, enabling a more expressive range of adaptive decay.
    *   **Implementation:** Added `context_decay_proj`, `conv_context_decay` (if `use_short_conv`), `context_decay_norm`, and `context_decay_scale`. The output of this new modulator adaptively scales the `g` projection alongside the existing `decay_modulator`.

### GatedSpikingMLP Enhancements:
1.  **Spiking Adaptive Convolutional Gate:** Within the `GatedSpikingMLP`, an adaptive spiking gate is introduced *before* the `ShortConvolution` layer. This gate's signal is derived from a separate `SpikingLinear` projection of the original input `x` to the MLP, followed by normalization and a sigmoid activation with a learnable scale. The resulting gate signal multiplicatively modulates the input to the `mlp_conv`. This design choice directly addresses the challenge of integrating non-spiking components (like `ShortConvolution`) more harmoniously into an SNN architecture. By making the convolution's input adaptively gated by a spiking signal, we enhance the 'SNN nature' of the block and provide 'Adaptive Computational Mechanisms' for local feature extraction within the MLP. This allows the MLP's local processing to dynamically adjust its sensitivity based on the input context.
    *   **Implementation:** Added `mlp_conv_gate_proj`, `mlp_conv_gate_norm`, and `mlp_conv_gate_scale`. The gate is applied to the output of `mlp_pre_conv_proj` before feeding into `mlp_conv`.

### Overall SNN Nature and Efficiency:
*   **Uniform SpikingLinear Usage:** All `nn.Linear` layers continue to be uniformly replaced by `SpikingLinear` to ensure that all inputs to linear transformations are binary spike signals, reinforcing the SNN characteristic of the network.
*   **Spike Collection:** All newly introduced `SpikingLinear` layers contribute their spike outputs to the `final_spikes` tensor, maintaining the required output format.
*   **Batch-Agnostic and Einops:** `einops.rearrange` is used consistently, and all tensor operations are designed to be batch-size independent and robust to dynamic shapes.
*   **Sub-quadratic Complexity:** The core `hgrn2_chunkwise` operator maintains its linear-time complexity, and the added gating mechanisms do not introduce quadratic overhead.

These modifications contribute to a more dynamic, context-aware, and neurologically plausible architecture, aiming for superior performance in sequence modeling tasks by enhancing the model's adaptive capabilities and multi-scale information processing within an efficient spiking framework.
