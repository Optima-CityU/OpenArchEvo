Idea:
The improved SpikingFLABlock, named `spiking_fla_sparse_activation`, focuses on enhancing the spiking neural network nature by promoting sparse spike activity and minimizing redundant analog activations. The primary modifications are: 

1.  **Re-introduction of Spike Regularization**: The `spike_reg_weight` parameter and the calculation of `reg_loss` have been brought back into the `SpikingFLABlock`. This encourages the network to learn more efficient and sparse spiking patterns, directly addressing the objective of reducing firing rates and fostering a more efficient spiking neural network nature. Sparse firing is a key characteristic of biological neural networks, leading to reduced energy consumption and potentially improved robustness.

2.  **Removal of Redundant Analog Activations**: The `F.gelu` activation functions previously present in the `SpikingMLP` and `SpikingResidualGate` modules (as seen in `Version 2`) have been removed. In a spiking neural network context, the non-linearity is primarily provided by the spiking neurons themselves within the `SpikingLinear` layers.
