Idea:
My motivation is to further enhance the neuromorphic nature of the SpikingFLABlock, particularly in how spike activity influences information flow, while maintaining the strong performance of the Version 2 baseline. The primary innovation is to refine the spike-driven residual gating mechanism for the attention block output.

### Implemented Changes:
1.  **Targeted Spike Aggregation for Residual Gating**: In the `SpikingFLABlock`'s `forward` method, the spike-driven residual gating (`self.spike_res_attn_proj`) has been modified. Instead of aggregating all available spikes from the `DeltaNet`'s internal projections, it now specifically leverages the spikes (`spikes_o`) generated by the `DeltaNet`'s final output projection (`self.o_proj`).
    *   **Rationale**: The spikes from `self.o_proj` represent the most processed and contextually relevant spike activity *after* the entire attention mechanism has operated. Using these spikes to modulate the residual connection provides a more direct, focused, and biologically plausible gating signal. This aligns with the neuromorphic principle of locality, where local outputs directly influence subsequent processing, and ensures that the gate is informed by the most salient features of the attention output, potentially leading to more adaptive and efficient information routing. It also implicitly encourages a sparser or more purposeful use of spike information for critical control signals.
2.  **Robust Spike Dimension Handling**: Ensured that the selected `o_proj_spikes` are consistently handled, explicitly unsqueezing a sequence dimension (`unsqueeze(1)`) if the input tensor is 2D (B, D) and converting to float before calculating the mean. This guarantees batch-size independence and robust operation across varying input shapes, as per the technical specifications.
