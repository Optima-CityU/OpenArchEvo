Idea:
This enhanced SpikingFLABlock, named `spiking_fla_holistic_gating`, refines the spike-gated residual connections by introducing a more holistic approach to modulating the MLP residual path. 

**Motivation for the change:**

1.  **Enhanced Intra-Block Spike Coordination:** In the previous version, the attention residual gate was modulated by attention-specific spikes (primarily `k_proj` spikes), and the MLP residual gate was modulated solely by MLP-specific spikes (`mlp_spike1`, `mlp_spike2`). While effective, this localized gating might not fully exploit the rich spatiotemporal information encoded in spikes across the entire block. By incorporating attention spikes (`k_proj`) into the MLP's residual gating mechanism, we enable the MLP to modulate its output based on a more comprehensive understanding of the block's overall neural activity, including the critical feature extraction occurring in the attention mechanism.

2.  **Deeper Integration of SNN Principles:** This modification promotes a more integrated "spiking-aware" information flow within the block. Instead of treating attention and MLP spike activities in isolation, their combined influence on the MLP's residual path encourages a more coherent and biologically plausible processing strategy. This can lead to improved learning dynamics and potentially more robust and sparse spike representations across the network.

3.  **Improved Flow Control and Regularization:** A more informed MLP residual gate, which considers both attention and MLP spike patterns, can lead to better regulation of information passing through the residual connection. This can help prevent runaway activity, stabilize training, and contribute to the network's ability to maintain efficient firing rates, especially when combined with the existing firing rate regularization.

**Implementation Details:**

-   The `SpikingMLP` class remains unchanged from [Version 2], retaining the `nn.GELU()` activation and `nn.Dropout()` for its beneficial non-linearity and regularization properties between `SpikingLinear` layers.
-   The core modification is within the `SpikingFLABlock`'s `forward` method, specifically how `mlp_block_spike_activity` is computed.
-   After the attention block computes its output and spikes, the `k_proj` spikes from the attention (`attn_spikes.get('k_proj')`) are explicitly saved.
-   When computing the `mlp_block_spike_activity` for the MLP residual gate, we now average the mean spike activities of `k_proj` (from attention), `mlp_spike1`, and `mlp_spike2`. This combined mean activity then feeds into the `self.mlp_spike_gate_proj` (a `SpikingLinear` layer) to generate the analog gating factor.

This approach ensures that all changes maintain sub-quadratic complexity, use `SpikingLinear()` uniformly, return `final_spikes` in the correct format, preserve all forward/init signatures, and are robust to varying batch sizes, adhering to all specified constraints.
