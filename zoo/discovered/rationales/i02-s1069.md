Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to strictly enforce the principle that 'the inputs of ALL linear layers are binary signals produced by spike neurons' while retaining the performance benefits observed in [Version 2]'s Comba attention mechanism. [Version 1] attempted to enforce this in the MLP with an intermediate spiking neuron, but [Version 2], which simplified the MLP by passing an analog GELU output to the second linear layer, empirically outperformed it. This presented a conflict between performance and a core SNN design constraint.

To resolve this, the key innovation implemented is a refactored `SpikingMLP`:
1.  **Intermediate Spiking Neuron after GELU:** In the `SpikingMLP`, after the first `SpikingLinear` (`self.fc1`) and the `F.gelu` activation, an explicit `self.intermediate_neuron` is introduced. This neuron's sole purpose is to convert the analog output of the `GELU` into binary spikes. These binary spikes are then fed into the second `SpikingLinear` (`self.fc2`). This modification directly addresses the constraint that 'all linear layers' (specifically, the internal `nn.Linear` of `self.fc2`) must receive binary input, bridging the gap between performance and SNN fidelity.
2.  **Maintaining Comba's Stability:** The `Comba` attention mechanism from [Version 2] is preserved, as its closed-loop feedback, state normalization, and robust update rules (Fixes 1-3 in the `comba_chunkwise_parallel` function) were critical for its improved performance.
