Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to enhance the 'efficient spiking neural network nature' and 'neuronmorphic computing natures' of the `SpikingFLABlock` by streamlining its activation functions. 

**Analysis of Previous Version's Limitations:**
In the previous version of `DeltaNet`, after the inputs `q`, `k`, and `v` were processed by `SpikingLinear` layers (which internally apply a spiking neuron before a linear projection) and potentially `ShortConvolution` layers (which were maintained in their ANN form), additional ANN-style activation functions (e.g., `F.silu`, `F.relu`, `elu_p1`) were applied. This created a hybrid and somewhat redundant application of non-linearities:
1.  `hidden_states` (real) -> `SpikingLinear` (neuron -> linear) -> `q_proj_output` (real-valued, derived from spikes).
2.  `q_proj_output` (real) -> `ShortConvolution` (ANN) -> `q_conv_output` (real).
3.  `q_conv_output` (real) -> `F.silu` (ANN activation) -> `q_activated` (real).

This sequence meant that a real-valued tensor, already having passed through a spiking non-linearity within `SpikingLinear`, was then subjected to another ANN-style non-linearity *before* entering the core `delta_rule_chunkwise` attention mechanism. This approach diminishes the purity and efficiency of an SNN-centric design, as it introduces non-spiking, potentially power-intensive computations where spike-driven non-linearity should ideally dominate.

**Proposed Architectural Evolution and Implementation:**
To address this, the evolved `DeltaNet` removes these redundant ANN-style activations, specifically:
1.  **Removal of post-convolutional/post-projection activations**: The explicit calls to `F.silu`, `q.relu()`, `elu_p1(k)`, etc., applied to `q`, `k`, and `v` after their initial `SpikingLinear` projections and `ShortConvolution` (if `use_short_conv` is true) have been removed from the `DeltaNet.forward` method. 
2.  **Streamlined `ShortConvolution` activation**: The `activation` parameter for the `ShortConvolution` layers (`q_conv1d`, `k_conv1d`, `v_conv1d`) in `DeltaNet.__init__` is now explicitly set to `None`. This ensures that `ShortConvolution` layers operate as purely linear convolutional filters, passing their real-valued outputs directly to the subsequent steps without introducing internal ANN non-linearities.
