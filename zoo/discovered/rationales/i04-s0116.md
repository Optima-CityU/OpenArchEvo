Idea:
The primary innovation is the introduction of a **Spike-Driven Residual Gating Mechanism** (`spike_res_attn_proj`) for the output of the attention block. This mechanism enhances the neuromorphic nature of the model by making the contribution of the attention's output to the residual connection dynamically dependent on the preceding spike activity within the attention mechanism itself.

**Motivation:**
1.  **Enhanced Neuromorphic Dynamics**: In biological neural networks, signal propagation and influence are heavily modulated by spike events. By using the aggregated spike activity from the attention mechanism's internal `SpikingLinear` layers to gate its own residual output, we create a more biologically plausible feedback loop. This means that if the attention computation (e.g., query, key, value projections, beta modulation) is highly active in terms of spiking, its output will have a stronger (or specifically modulated) impact on the subsequent layers, and vice-versa.
2.  **Adaptive Information Flow**: This gating mechanism allows the model to adaptively control the flow of information. If certain parts of the sequence or specific features exhibit low spiking activity within the attention block, their contribution via the residual path can be attenuated, effectively focusing the downstream layers on more "active" or salient information channels as determined by the spiking dynamics. This can lead to more efficient processing and potentially better signal-to-noise ratios.
3.  **Local Contextual Control**: The aggregation of spikes happens per token position (`(B, L, 1)`), making the gating context-dependent along the sequence length. This allows for fine-grained control over how each token's attention output is integrated into the overall sequence representation, driven by its own spiking activity.
4.  **Preservation of Hybrid Architecture**: The modification seamlessly integrates into the existing SNN-ANN hybrid architecture. It leverages the binary spike outputs from `SpikingLinear` layers, processes them through another `SpikingLinear` layer, and uses the resulting analog signal (after an activation) as a gate, maintaining the principle that all `nn.Linear` layers are replaced by `SpikingLinear` and spike signals are utilized.
5.  **Computational Efficiency**: The spike aggregation and a single `SpikingLinear` layer for the gate add minimal computational overhead (linear in sequence length and model dimension), thus preserving the sub-quadratic complexity of the overall block. Spikes are binary, making their aggregation and processing inherently efficient.
