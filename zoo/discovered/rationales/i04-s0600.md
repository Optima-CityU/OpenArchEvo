Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to enhance the neuromorphic characteristics and adaptive capabilities of the SpikingFLABlock, building upon the performance gains observed in Version 2. Version 2 demonstrated that simplifying the SpikingMLP (by removing non-spiking activation and dropout between linear layers) and removing dropout in the main block's residual connections led to improved performance. This suggests that a more direct propagation of analog signals through spiking linear layers, with non-linearities primarily arising from the spiking neurons themselves, is beneficial.

Our key innovation focuses on refining the `beta` modulation mechanism within the `DeltaNet` attention block. In prior versions, `beta` was modulated by the mean spike activity of only the `key` projection (`k_spike_mean_activity`). While effective, this approach relies on a single source of spike-driven feedback. To promote 'Multi-Scale Information Integration' and 'Adaptive Computational Mechanisms' (as per the innovation target domains), we propose a more comprehensive spike-driven modulation:

1.  **Combined Spike Activity for Beta Modulation**: Instead of using only `spikes_k_proj`, we now calculate `combined_spike_activity` by averaging the binary spikes from the `q_proj`, `k_proj`, and `v_proj` layers. This aggregate signal provides a richer, more holistic representation of the instantaneous activity across the entire attention input mechanism. By integrating spike information from query, key, and value streams, the `beta` modulator gains a broader context, allowing it to adaptively scale the attention based on the overall excitability and feature richness of the input.

2.  **Neuromorphic Efficiency**: This modification directly aligns with neuromorphic computing principles by making a critical attention parameter (`beta`) explicitly dependent on the collective spike activity within the network. This 'spike-aware' adaptation can lead to more efficient information processing, potentially influencing the subsequent firing rates and overall energy consumption by dynamically adjusting the 'gain' of the value stream. It encourages a clean, stateful structure where the flow of information is modulated by the very spiking events that constitute the computation.

3.  **Reinforcement of Version 2's Simplifications**: The architectural choices from Version 2, specifically the removal of non-spiking GELU activation and dropout layers within `SpikingMLP`, and the removal of dropout layers in the main `SpikingFLABlock`'s residual paths, are retained. This reinforces the design philosophy of moving towards a more streamlined, spiking-centric architecture where the spiking neurons themselves provide the necessary non-linearity and regularization, reducing reliance on conventional ANN components where possible. This simplifies the data flow and aligns with the objective of using 'plausible operators' and maintaining 'high cohesion and low coupling'.

These changes are implemented while strictly adhering to all technical constraints, including sub-quadratic complexity, chunked processing, causal integrity, `einops.rearrange()` for tensor manipulations, batch-size agnostic operations, and the preservation of the `SpikingLinear` class and existing method signatures. The `final_spikes` tensor still correctly aggregates all binary spike outputs from every `SpikingLinear` layer.
