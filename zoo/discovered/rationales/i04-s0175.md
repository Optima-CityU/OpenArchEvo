Idea:
The primary motivation for this evolution is to enhance the neuromorphic computing characteristics of the SpikingFLABlock by explicitly promoting **spike sparsity**, a critical aspect for energy efficiency and biological plausibility in Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs). While the existing architecture correctly integrates `SpikingLinear` layers to ensure that all internal `nn.Linear` operations process binary spikes, it does not explicitly regularize the overall firing activity of the network. High firing rates in SNNs can diminish their energy-efficiency benefits and potentially lead to less sparse representations.

The implemented change introduces a **spike activity regularization loss** within both the `DeltaNet` attention mechanism and the `SpikingMLP`. This regularization term penalizes excessive firing by calculating the mean firing rate (average spike activity) across all generated spikes.

**Implementation Details:**
1.  **`DeltaNet` Spike Regularization:**
    *   A `spike_reg_weight` parameter (defaulting to 0.01) is added to `DeltaNet`'s `__init__` via `kwargs`.
    *   In `DeltaNet`'s `forward` method, after collecting spikes from all `SpikingLinear` layers into `all_spikes`, a `reg_loss` is computed. This `reg_loss` is the sum of the mean spike activity (average of binary spike tensors converted to float) from each `spike_tensor` in `all_spikes`, scaled by `spike_reg_weight`.
    *   `DeltaNet`'s `forward` now explicitly returns this computed `reg_loss` (which aligns with its existing `Optional[torch.Tensor]` return signature for regularization loss).

2.  **`SpikingFLABlock` Global Regularization:**
    *   A `spike_reg_weight` parameter (defaulting to 0.01) is added to `SpikingFLABlock`'s `__init__` via `kwargs` for consistency.
    *   In `SpikingFLABlock`'s `forward` method, the `attn_reg_loss` returned by `DeltaNet` is accumulated into a `total_reg_loss`.
    *   Additionally, after the `SpikingMLP` returns its `mlp_spike1` and `mlp_spike2`, their individual mean firing rates are calculated and added to the `total_reg_loss`, also scaled by `spike_reg_weight`. This ensures that the MLP's firing activity is also regularized.
    *   The `SpikingFLABlock` then returns this `total_reg_loss` as its `reg_loss` output.
