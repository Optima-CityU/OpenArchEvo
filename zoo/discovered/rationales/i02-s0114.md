Idea:
The primary goal of this evolution is to further enhance the neuromorphic nature and reduce the reliance on non-spiking operations within the `SpikingFLABlock`, specifically in the `SpikingMLP` component, while leveraging the superior performance of the GLA attention mechanism identified in prior experiments. 

**Key Implementation:**
1.  **Retention of GLA:** The `GLA` (Gated Linear Attention) mechanism, which has empirically shown better performance, is retained as the core attention module within `SpikingFLABlock`. Its chunkwise parallelization and efficient recurrent state management ensure sub-quadratic complexity and effective long-range dependency modeling.
2.  **SpikingMLP Enhancement:** The most significant modification is within the `SpikingMLP` class. Traditionally, a non-spiking activation function like `GELU` is used between the two linear layers. To adhere more strictly to the spiking neural network paradigm and encourage efficient spiking nature, the `F.gelu` activation has been replaced by an explicit intermediate spiking neuron (`self.intermediate_neuron`). This ensures that the input to the second `SpikingLinear` layer (`self.fc2`) is directly derived from the binary spikes produced by the intermediate neuron, thereby increasing the spike-driven computation throughout the MLP. This change aligns with the principle of ensuring inputs to all linear layers are binary signals produced by spike neurons, as `SpikingLinear` already wraps its internal `nn.Linear` to operate on spikes.
3.  **Comprehensive Spike Collection:** The `SpikingFLABlock`'s `forward` method has been updated to correctly collect and concatenate all generated spike tensors from both the `GLA` attention module and the enhanced `SpikingMLP` (including the newly introduced intermediate spikes) into a single `final_spikes` output. This fulfills the requirement for returning a complete, flat tensor of binary spikes.

**Theoretical Grounding and Expected Benefits:**
The replacement of `GELU` with an intermediate spiking neuron directly enhances the model's neuromorphic characteristics. By promoting spike-based communication between layers in the MLP, we aim to:
*   **Reduce Analog Computations:** Minimizing the use of continuous, non-spiking activations like `GELU` helps in moving towards a more biologically plausible and energy-efficient spiking computation model.
*   **Increase Spiking Activity Locality:** Ensuring spike-driven input to subsequent linear layers fosters a more localized and event-driven information processing, which is a hallmark of SNNs. This could potentially lead to reduced firing rates by encouraging more sparse, information-rich spikes.
