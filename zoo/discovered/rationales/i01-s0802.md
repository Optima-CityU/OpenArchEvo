Idea:
The evolution from Version 1 to Version 2 introduced several key improvements, notably simplified residual connections and more sophisticated adaptive gating mechanisms in `SpikingHGRN_Adaptive`, leading to better performance. This new version, `spiking_fla_adaptive_local_gates`, builds upon the successful `SpikingFLABlock` from Version 2 with the following innovations:

1.  **Enhanced Local Context for Adaptive Modulators in SpikingHGRN_Adaptive**:
    *   **Problem**: In Version 2, the `decay_modulator` and `input_modulator` directly receive processed linear projections (`proj_out`) before normalization and activation. While effective, this setup doesn't explicitly leverage local temporal dependencies within the sequence for these crucial adaptive gating signals. Research in sequence modeling often highlights the importance of local context for robust information processing.
    *   **Solution**: We introduce `ShortConvolution` layers (`conv_decay_modulator` and `conv_input_modulator`) to the pathways of both `decay_modulator_raw` and `input_modulator_raw` *before* their respective RMS normalization and sigmoid activation.
