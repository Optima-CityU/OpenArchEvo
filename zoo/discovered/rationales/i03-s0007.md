Idea:
The primary goal of this evolution is to simplify the overall architecture by streamlining activation flows and enhancing the adherence to a more pure spiking neural network (SNN) paradigm where feasible, while retaining critical hybrid (SNN-ANN) components that have shown performance benefits. This design seeks to leverage the strengths observed in [Version 2] while addressing inconsistencies and promoting a cleaner, more biologically plausible computational model.

Key architectural innovations and their implementation:

1.  **Streamlined `DeltaNet` Beta Modulation:**
    *   **Implementation:** The `spike_beta_mod_proj` (a `SpikingLinear` layer) and its related modulation logic from [Version 1] has been removed in `DeltaNet`'s `__init__` and `forward` methods. This aligns with the simplification observed in [Version 2].
    *   **Motivation:** The `spike_beta_mod_proj` introduced an additional modulation layer based on mean spike activity for the `beta` parameter. Removing this simplifies the `beta` computation path, reducing the number of parameters and potentially improving stability by relying solely on the directly learned `b_proj` which is already a `SpikingLinear` layer.
