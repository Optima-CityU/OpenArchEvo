The evolution from Version 1 to this enhanced architecture, named `spiking_fla_adaptive_gating`, is driven by a two-pronged strategy: deepening neuromorphic integration through spike-driven adaptive mechanisms and re-introducing proven regularization techniques for improved generalization.

### Key Architectural Innovations and Their Implementation:

1.  **Spike-Driven Output Gating in `DeltaNet` (Neuromorphic Integration):**
    *   **Motivation**: Version 2 introduced spike-based modulation for `beta`, a step towards more neuromorphic control. Extending this, we now make the output gating mechanism in `DeltaNet` directly responsive to the neural activity, specifically the spiking activity of the value projections (`spikes_v_proj`). This emulates biological principles where neural outputs are modulated based on the salience or activity of incoming signals. By using the mean spike activity of `v` (the information content flowing through the value pathway) to generate the gate, the network gains an adaptive mechanism to control the flow of information at the normalization stage.
