This directly implements the finding that explicit mean firing rate regularization, as in Version 1, was detrimental to overall performance. Consequently, `total_reg_loss` will consistently be `None`.
2.  **Enforced Full Spiking MLP Usage**: The `use_spiking_mlp` boolean flag in `SpikingFLABlock`'s `__init__` has been removed. Instead, the `SpikingMLP` is now always instantiated directly.
