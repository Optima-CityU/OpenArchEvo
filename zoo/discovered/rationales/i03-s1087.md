Idea:
The primary innovation in this version is the introduction of a spike-gated mechanism within the SpikingMLP block. Building upon the observation that re-introducing `F.gelu` significantly improved performance in [Version 2] compared to [Version 1], we aim to leverage this continuous non-linearity while simultaneously enhancing the inherent spiking nature and encouraging sparsity in the intermediate representations.

**Implemented Changes:**
1.  **Spike-Gated MLP Hidden State:** A new spiking neuron (`self.gating_neuron`) is introduced within the `SpikingMLP` module. This neuron is applied directly to the continuous output of the `F.gelu` activation, producing binary `gating_spikes` with the same shape as the MLP's hidden dimension (`d_ffn`).
2.  **Sparsity Enforcement:** These `gating_spikes` are then used to perform an element-wise multiplication with the `gelu`-activated continuous output (`x_gelu`). This multiplication effectively gates the information flow, zeroing out hidden units where the `gating_neuron` did not fire. This mechanism enforces a spike-driven sparsity in the MLP's hidden state, aligning with the principles of efficient neuromorphic computing by reducing redundant activity.
3.  **Spike Collection:** The `gating_spikes` are collected alongside other spikes from the `SpikingLinear` layers and are included in the `final_spikes` tensor returned by the `SpikingFLABlock`.

*   **Enhanced SNN Nature:** By introducing an additional spike-generating neuron that directly influences the continuous hidden state, we bridge the gap between continuous ANN-like processing (GELU) and discrete SNN operations.
