Idea:
The evolutionary design focuses on enhancing the spiking neural network's adaptive capabilities and numerical stability, directly addressing critical performance limitations observed in sequence modeling tasks. This is achieved through three core innovations:

1.  **Enhanced Recurrent State Stability (DeltaNet Integration)**:
    *   **Problem Addressed**: Unbounded recurrent states in sequential models, especially in long-sequence scenarios, can lead to numerical instability, gradient vanishing/exploding, and diminished long-term memory fidelity. This is particularly problematic in SNNs where sparse activity can exacerbate these issues.
    *   **Innovation**: Introduced state clamping (`state_clamp_val`) within the `delta_rule_chunkwise` operator of the `DeltaNet`. This mechanism constrains the magnitude of the recurrent state `S` after each update, ensuring it remains within a stable, predefined range.
    *   **Theoretical Grounding**: Similar to gradient clipping or weight regularization, state clamping provides a form of inductive bias that promotes numerical stability and robust learning, especially critical for models with long-term dependencies. It helps in maintaining a healthy dynamic range for the state, preventing information loss or saturation.

2.  **Adaptive Spike-Driven Gating with Learnable Modulation**:
    *   **Problem Addressed**: Fixed-range gating mechanisms (e.g., simple sigmoid) might lack the expressiveness to dynamically modulate information flow based on varying spike activities effectively. The network needs finer control over how spike presence influences its processing.
    *   **Innovation**: Replaced the simple `sigmoid` activation for `attn_head_gate_proj` and `mlp_gate_proj` with an adaptive modulation factor: `1.0 + F.softplus(self.gate_gain) * torch.sigmoid(raw_gating_output + self.gate_bias)`. This introduces learnable `gate_gain` (ensured positive by `softplus`) and `gate_bias` parameters.
    *   **Theoretical Grounding**: This adaptive gating allows the network to learn both the magnitude (`gate_gain`) and the threshold (`gate_bias`) of modulation based on incoming spike activity. The `1.0 + ...` structure ensures a minimum scaling factor, preventing complete information blockage while allowing for learned amplification or suppression.
