Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution is to enhance the intrinsic spiking neural network (SNN) nature of the `SpikingFLABlock` by simplifying the `SpikingMLP` component. 

**Observed Limitation (from Version 2 analysis):** While Version 2 made significant strides in integrating spiking neurons and spike-driven mechanisms, the `SpikingMLP` still included a standard Artificial Neural Network (ANN) activation function (`nn.GELU`) between its two `SpikingLinear` layers. This introduced an additional non-linearity that is not directly derived from the spiking dynamics, somewhat diluting the pure neuromorphic character of the MLP.
