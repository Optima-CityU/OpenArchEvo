Idea:
The primary objective of this evolution is to further align the SpikingFLABlock with neuromorphic computing principles by reducing redundant non-linearities and encouraging a more "SNN-native" computation flow. Version 2 introduced `SpikingLinear` layers that internally convert analog inputs to spikes for the `nn.Linear` operation, and then output analog values. However, `SpikingMLP` and `SpikingResidualGate` then applied `F.gelu` (an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) activation function) to these analog outputs before feeding them into subsequent `SpikingLinear` layers. 

This modification removes the explicit `F.gelu` activation functions from both the `SpikingMLP` and `SpikingResidualGate` modules. The rationale is that the `SpikingLinear` layer's internal spiking neuron already provides a non-linear transformation. By removing `F.gelu`, we rely solely on the inherent non-linearity of the spiking neuron for activation, simplifying the computational graph and making the block more consistent with a purely spiking neural network paradigm. This change aims to: 

1.  **Enhance SNN Purity**: Remove ANN-specific activation functions to ensure that non-linearity is primarily derived from the spiking mechanism, fostering a more biologically plausible and energy-efficient computational model. 
2.  **Simplify Architecture**: Reduce architectural complexity by eliminating redundant activation layers. 
3.  **Encourage Spiking Dynamics**: Emphasize the role of spike generation and propagation as the core computational primitives. 

This change maintains all critical constraints, including sub-quadratic complexity, batch size independence, chunkwise processing, and the correct `final_spikes` output format, while pushing the architecture towards a cleaner and more SNN-centric design.
