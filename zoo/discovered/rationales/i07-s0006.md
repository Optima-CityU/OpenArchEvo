Idea:
The evolution from Version 1 to Version 2 highlighted the superior performance of a Mamba-like state-space model (`SpikingMamba2`) combined with a novel spike-activity modulated residual gating mechanism (`SpikingResidualGate`). This indicates that efficient state-based recurrence, when properly integrated with spiking neurons, offers significant advantages for sequence modeling in SNNs. My design builds upon the proven foundation of Version 2, focusing on refining its neuromorphic properties and enhancing explicit control over spike dynamics while strictly adhering to the specified constraints.

### Key Architectural Innovations and Their Implementation:

1.  **Reinforced Spike-Activity-Based Residual Gating (`SpikingResidualGate`):**
    *   **Innovation**: The `SpikingResidualGate`, introduced in Version 2, dynamically modulates the residual connection based on the mean firing activity of the preceding spiking layers within a block. This mechanism is crucial for SNNs, as it allows for adaptive information flow and implicitly encourages spike sparsity, thereby reducing unnecessary computation and energy consumption, which aligns with neuromorphic principles. The gate itself uses `SpikingLinear` layers, ensuring that its internal computation is also spike-driven.
    *   **Implementation**: This module remains central. It takes the average spike activity across a block's spiking layers (e.g., `in_proj`, `out_proj` from `SpikingMamba2`, or `fc1`, `fc2` from `SpikingMLP`), processes it through two `SpikingLinear` layers with `F.gelu` activation, and outputs a `modulation_factor` between 1.0 and 3.0 via `1.0 + 2.0 * torch.sigmoid(mod_factor_raw + self.bias)`. This factor then scales the output of the attention or MLP block before it's added to the residual. This adaptive scaling helps the network focus on salient information or control activity levels based on the neuron's collective state.

2.  **Explicit Firing Rate Regularization (`spike_regularization_weight`):**
    *   **Innovation**: Re-introducing explicit firing rate regularization, a best practice from Version 1, provides direct control over the overall spike sparsity and energy efficiency of the SNN. While the `SpikingResidualGate` offers adaptive, local control, global regularization prevents excessive firing across the entire model, which is essential for stable SNN training and hardware deployment.
    *   **Implementation**: A `spike_regularization_weight` parameter (defaulting to `0.01`) is added to `SpikingFLABlock.__init__`. In the `forward` pass, after collecting all `final_spikes` from every spiking layer, the mean firing rate is calculated (`final_spikes.float().mean()`), scaled by `spike_regularization_weight`, and returned as `total_reg_loss`. This ensures that the training objective directly penalizes high firing rates, leading to more energy-efficient and biologically plausible SNNs.

3.  **Robust `SpikingMamba2` Integration for Sequence Modeling:**
    *   **Innovation**: `SpikingMamba2` (based on the efficient Mamba-2 SSM architecture) replaces the `DeltaNet` from Version 1. Mamba's parallel scan mechanism offers `O(N)` computational complexity for sequence length `N`, effectively modeling long-range dependencies while maintaining causality. By wrapping all internal linear projections (`in_proj`, `out_proj`) with `SpikingLinear`, the core computations are fed binary spikes, adhering to the SNN nature.
    *   **Implementation**: The `SpikingMamba2` module is preserved as in Version 2, including the `mamba2_ssd_parallel_stable` operator for chunked, parallel recurrence. `dt_bias` is initialized negatively (`-2.0`) to promote long-term memory at the start of training. The module correctly handles `past_key_values` for recurrent states and `conv_state` for the `ShortConvolution` layer, ensuring compatibility with incremental decoding and cache utilization. Minor robustness improvements, such as ensuring `dtype` consistency for `initial_state` in `mamba2_ssd_parallel_stable` and explicit `use_cache` propagation to `ShortConvolution`, have been added.

### Connection between Theory, Evidence, and Implementation:

*   **Evidence**: Version 2's superior performance suggests that efficient state-space models (`SpikingMamba2`) are better suited for sequence modeling than previous FLA-based approaches in a spiking context. The introduction of `SpikingResidualGate` further improved performance, indicating the value of dynamic, spike-driven modulation.
*   **Theory (Neuromorphic Computing)**: Spiking Neural Networks are characterized by sparse, event-driven computation. Firing rate regularization directly encourages this sparsity, reducing power consumption and making the model more amenable to neuromorphic hardware. The `SpikingResidualGate` leverages the distinct signal characteristics of SNNs (spike activity) to implement a biologically plausible gating mechanism, promoting adaptive learning and information gating based on neural activity levels. The state-based nature of `SpikingMamba2` (local recurrence with global context) mimics neuronal memory and temporal integration, crucial for sequence processing.
*   **Implementation**: All linear transformations are wrapped in `SpikingLinear`, which uses a spiking neuron to generate binary signals before feeding them to the underlying `nn.Linear` layers. This ensures a consistent SNN-driven data path. `einops.rearrange()` is used throughout for batch-size agnostic and readable tensor manipulations. Chunked processing in `mamba2_ssd_parallel_stable` guarantees sub-quadratic complexity (`O(N)`). The combined approach offers a robust, efficient, and neuromorphically-inspired architecture for advanced sequence modeling.
