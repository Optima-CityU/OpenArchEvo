Idea:
The `spiking_fla_rate_regularized` architecture introduces an explicit firing rate regularization mechanism to the `SpikingFLABlock`, enhancing its neuromorphic computing nature and providing a direct control knob for optimizing spiking activity. 

**1. Enhanced SNN-Native Optimization through Firing Rate Regularization:**
This version integrates a `spike_regularization_weight` parameter, which is used to compute a loss term based on the mean firing rate of all collected binary spikes across the block. By penalizing high firing rates, the model is encouraged to learn more energy-efficient and biologically plausible sparse spiking patterns. This directly addresses the `neuromorphic computing natures` objective of considering structures that might be beneficial for `reducing the firing rate`.

**2. Explicit Control over SNN Behavior:**
The `spike_regularization_weight` allows researchers and practitioners to explicitly tune the trade-off between model performance and spike activity sparsity. This fine-grained control is crucial for developing robust SNNs, as uncontrolled firing rates can lead to stability issues or inefficient computation.

**3. Improved Training Stability and Generalization:**
Firing rate regularization acts as a form of implicit regularization. By limiting excessive neuronal activity, it can help mitigate issues like vanishing or exploding gradients, contributing to more stable training, especially in deeper SNNs. This can lead to better generalization capabilities by preventing the network from relying on overly dense or high-frequency firing.

**4. Seamless Integration within Hybrid ANN-SNN Design:**
The regularization is applied to the aggregated `final_spikes` output from both the `DeltaNet` and `SpikingMLP` components. This allows for a holistic optimization of spiking activity across the entire block without altering the core mechanisms of `SpikingLinear`, `DeltaNet`, or `SpikingMLP`. It maintains the successful hybrid ANN-SNN approach while pushing towards greater SNN efficiency.

**5. Strict Adherence to Technical and Architectural Constraints:**
All critical constraints are meticulously upheld, including:
*   **Preservation of `SpikingLinear` class:** The `SpikingLinear` class remains untouched.
*   **Uniform use of `SpikingLinear`:** All `nn.Linear` instances are wrapped by `SpikingLinear` to ensure binary spike inputs.
*   **Correct Spike Output Format:** A single flattened `final_spikes` tensor is returned, representing all binary spikes.
*   **Class Structure and Signatures:** `SpikingFLABlock` name, `__init__` with `**kwargs`, and `forward` method signature are preserved.
*   **Sub-quadratic Complexity:** The underlying `delta_rule_chunkwise` attention mechanism maintains its O(N) or O(N log N) complexity.
*   **Chunked Processing and Causal Integrity:** These are maintained through the `delta_rule_chunkwise` implementation.
*   **`einops.rearrange()`:** All `.view()`/`.reshape()` operations are replaced with `einops.rearrange()` for robustness and clarity.
*   **Batch Size Agnosticism:** The code is designed to operate correctly with any batch size, avoiding hardcoded dimensions.
*   **No `@torch.compile` or `bf.float16`:** These decorators are explicitly avoided.
