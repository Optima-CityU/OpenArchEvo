### Motivation for SpikingFLABlock_DualGatedMLP

This architectural evolution focuses on enhancing the neuromorphic nature and sparsity within the Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) block, a critical component of the `SpikingFLABlock`. While the existing architecture ([Version 2]) correctly integrates `SpikingLinear` layers to ensure that all `nn.Linear` operations receive binary spike inputs, the intermediate signal flow within the MLP remained largely continuous, with a single spike-gating mechanism applied *after* the GELU activation. The `SpikingFLABlock_DualGatedMLP` introduces an additional layer of spike-based gating to promote a more sparse and efficient spiking activity pattern.
