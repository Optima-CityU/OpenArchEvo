Idea:
This architectural evolution introduces two key enhancements to the SpikingFLABlock, building upon the foundations laid in Version 2, to further improve the efficient spiking neural network nature and model performance:

1.  **Enhanced Beta Modulation via Combined Spike Activity (within `DeltaNet`):** Version 2 introduced spike-based modulation of the `beta` parameter using only the mean spike activity of the `k_proj` output. This new version refines this mechanism by incorporating *both* the mean spike activity from `q_proj` and `k_proj` outputs. By concatenating these two mean activities, a richer, more comprehensive representation of the instantaneous attention activity is provided to the `spike_beta_mod_proj` layer. This allows `beta` to be more adaptively and dynamically controlled by the overall spike-driven interaction between queries and keys. This aligns with neuromorphic principles where distributed neural activity can influence synaptic dynamics or gain control, leading to more robust and context-aware recurrent memory interactions.

2.  **Re-introduction of Firing Rate Regularization (within `SpikingFLABlock`):** A critical aspect of efficient spiking neural networks is maintaining low firing rates to reduce computational cost and energy consumption. This version re-introduces a `spike_regularization_weight` in the `SpikingFLABlock`'s `__init__` method and applies a firing rate regularization loss across *all* collected binary spikes from the block (attention and MLP sub-layers). This regularization term explicitly encourages the model to learn sparse spike patterns, which is a direct objective for SNN efficiency and biological plausibility, preventing excessive and potentially redundant neural activity.
