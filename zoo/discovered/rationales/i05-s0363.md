### Motivation for Spiking Gated FLA Block

This evolution of the `SpikingFLABlock` from Version 1 introduces a crucial architectural enhancement: **Spiking Gating for the State Space Model (SS4D) branch**. This modification is driven by the core objectives of improving neuromorphic computing characteristics, enhancing adaptive computational mechanisms, and further integrating spiking dynamics into the control flow of the network, all while adhering to strict efficiency and implementation constraints.
