Idea:
The evolution from Version 1 to Version 2 highlighted the importance of properly ordering the ShortConvolution and SpikingLinear layers within the residual gating mechanism, with Version 2, which applies ShortConvolution directly to the continuous residual sum before the SpikingLinear gates, showing superior performance. Building upon this insight, this evolved architecture further refines the handling of continuous signals within the Spiking Neural Network (SNN) context.

The core architectural innovation in this version is the strategic insertion of `RMSNorm` layers immediately after *all* initial `SpikingLinear` projections within the `SpikingMesaNet` module. Specifically, `RMSNorm` is applied to the continuous outputs (`q_cont`, `k_cont`, `v_cont`, `eta_cont`, `decay_cont`) that emerge from the `SpikingLinear` projection layers, *before* these signals are passed to subsequent operations like `ShortConvolution` or activation functions (`torch.sigmoid`).

**Motivation for this change:**
1.  **Membrane Potential/Rate Normalization:** In SNNs, the continuous outputs of spiking neurons (after their internal linear transformation) can be interpreted as either membrane potentials or rate-coded signals. Normalizing these signals immediately after generation helps in controlling their dynamic range and preventing runaway activity or saturation, which is crucial for stable training and robust performance in deep SNNs.
2.  **Firing Rate Control:** By stabilizing the input scale to subsequent `ShortConvolution` layers and activation functions, these `RMSNorm` layers indirectly contribute to regulating the overall firing rate of downstream spiking neurons. Well-scaled inputs are less likely to cause excessive or sparse spiking, promoting more efficient and informative spike trains.
3.  **Enhanced Signal Consistency:** Normalizing key signals (`q`, `k`, `v`, `eta`, `decay`) ensures that the `mesa_chunkwise_parallel` operator receives consistently scaled inputs, which can improve the stability and learning dynamics of the attention mechanism, especially given its dependence on online gradient-descent-like updates.
4.  **Adherence to SNN Principles:** This modification reinforces the neuromorphic computing nature by emphasizing careful management of continuous representations (membrane potentials) that drive spike generation. It aligns with the principle of maintaining controlled signal propagation while leveraging the computational benefits of spiking dynamics.

This design maintains all critical constraints, including sub-quadratic complexity, chunked processing, batch-size independence, and strict adherence to the `SpikingLinear` interface to ensure all internal `nn.Linear` layers process binary spike signals. The `SpikingMLP` already incorporated similar normalization (`self.norm_mid`), so this change brings the `SpikingMesaNet`'s internal continuous signal handling in line with best practices for SNN stability.
