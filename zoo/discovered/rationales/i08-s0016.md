Idea:
This evolved `SpikingFLABlock` leverages the robust and efficient Structured State Space Sequence (S4D) model as its core mechanism, directly addressing limitations of quadratic complexity and enabling superior long-range dependency modeling. The previous `Comba` operator, while innovative, relied on chunk-wise attention-like mechanisms that, despite efforts to optimize, introduced architectural complexity and potential state explosion issues. By adopting `SS4D`, we inherently gain sub-quadratic complexity (typically O(N log N) or O(N) due to FFT-based convolutions) and a powerful recurrent state-space formulation, which is naturally suited for modeling continuous-time dynamics—a fundamental aspect of neuromorphic computing.
