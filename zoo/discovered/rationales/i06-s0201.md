Idea:
The primary innovation in this version of the `SpikingFLABlock` is the introduction of a more refined spike activity regularization mechanism. Building upon the existing spike collection and an L1 regularization approach, this update allows for controlling the overall spiking activity by targeting a specific firing rate.

**What was implemented:**
1.  **`target_firing_rate` parameter:** Added a new parameter `target_firing_rate` (defaulting to 0.05) to the `SpikingFLABlock`'s `__init__` method. This parameter defines the desired mean firing rate for all collected spikes within the block.
2.  **`spike_reg_type` parameter:** Introduced `spike_reg_type` (defaulting to 'l2_target') to allow selection between L1 regularization (penalizing the mean firing rate directly, as in the previous version) and a new L2 regularization that encourages the mean firing rate to converge towards the `target_firing_rate`.
3.  **Modified Regularization Loss Calculation:** In the `SpikingFLABlock.forward` method, if `spike_reg_weight` is greater than 0, the `total_reg_loss` is now calculated based on `spike_reg_type`:
    *   If `'l1'`, it behaves as before: `spike_activity_mean * self.spike_reg_weight`.
    *   If `'l2_target'`, it calculates `(spike_activity_mean - self.target_firing_rate).pow(2) * self.spike_reg_weight`.

**Why this was implemented and its expected benefits:**
This enhancement directly addresses the objective of generating excellent spiking neural networks for sequence modeling tasks by providing finer control over the network's spiking dynamics. Traditional L1 regularization simply pushes spike activity downwards, potentially leading to overly sparse networks that struggle with information propagation. By introducing an L2 regularization towards a `target_firing_rate`, we can:
*   **Encourage Optimal Sparsity:** Maintain a healthy balance of spiking activity, preventing both excessive, energy-inefficient firing and critically low activity that could lead to vanishing gradients or loss of information.
*   **Improve Training Stability:** Guiding the average firing rate to a specific target can make training more stable and predictable, especially for deep SNNs.
*   **Enhance Cognitive Potential:** A well-regulated spiking activity pattern can lead to more robust and expressive neural representations, potentially improving the model's capacity for complex sequence modeling tasks like those found in ListOps or WikiText2.
