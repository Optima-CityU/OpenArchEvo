Idea:
The primary motivation for this architectural evolution stems from the goal of enhancing the spiking neural network (SNN) nature of the SpikingFLABlock, particularly within the Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) component, to promote computational efficiency and align more closely with neuromorphic computing principles. 

**Analysis of Previous Version (Version 2):**
The `SpikingMLP` in `Version 2` introduced an `nn.GELU()` activation function after the first `SpikingLinear` layer and before the second. While `SpikingLinear` ensures that its internal `nn.Linear` receives binary spikes (as per the core requirement), the `nn.GELU()` operates on the *analog* output of the first `SpikingLinear`'s internal `nn.Linear`. This means that `SpikingMLP` was introducing an additional, non-spiking, purely Artificial Neural Network (ANN) activation in its intermediate computation path. 

**Justification for the Implemented Change:**
The key modification involves the removal of the `nn.GELU()` activation from the `SpikingMLP` in the `SpikingFLABlock`. This change is justified by the following:

1.  **Emphasis on Spiking Non-linearity**: Spiking neurons inherently provide a non-linear thresholding operation. By removing the `nn.GELU()`, the `SpikingMLP` relies solely on the non-linearity introduced by the `src.models.spike.neuron` within each `SpikingLinear` layer. This aligns more directly with the objective of building efficient spiking neural networks and reduces the reliance on traditional ANN components for non-linearity.
