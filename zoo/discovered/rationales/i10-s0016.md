This includes the `feedback_net` for dynamic state modulation and the critical stability enhancements: normalization of state features, limiting feedback offset (`tanh * 2.0`), careful handling of decay for `decay_mask`, and strict renormalization of the recurrent state `S` after each chunk update (`S = S / torch.clamp(S_norm_per_head / 10.0, min=1.0)`).
    *   **Motivation:** Experimental evidence suggests the `Comba` operator significantly outperforms its predecessor. Its closed-loop recurrent nature, where a dedicated `feedback_net` processes the internal state (`S`) to modulate the gate (`g`), provides an adaptive and context-sensitive mechanism for information flow. The stability enhancements are paramount for training deep SNNs, preventing issues like exploding hidden states and enabling effective long-term memory integration, a common challenge in recurrent architectures.
