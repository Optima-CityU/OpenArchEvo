Idea:
The primary innovation in this version of `SpikingFLABlock` is the refinement of the spike-dependent gating mechanism for residual connections in both the attention and MLP sub-blocks. 

**1. Direct Binary Spike-Driven Gating:**
Instead of modulating the attention and MLP outputs with `sigmoid(analog_output)` from the gating `SpikingLinear` projection, this version directly uses the *binary spikes* generated by the gating `SpikingLinear` layer. The gating factor is computed as `(1 + learnable_scale * binary_spike)`. This makes the gating mechanism more explicitly and directly dependent on the occurrence of binary spike events rather than continuous analog activations, aligning better with neuromorphic computing principles.

**2. Enhanced Neuromorphic Alignment:**
By directly utilizing binary spikes for gating, the network's internal dynamics become more 'spiking-native'. This reduces the reliance on traditional analog operations (like `sigmoid` and subsequent analog multiplication) for critical control paths. This simplification supports the objective of encouraging SNN operations and discouraging complex ANN-like operations, promoting a cleaner structure with high cohesion and low coupling in a hybrid SNN-ANN design.

**3. Simplified Logic and Learnability:**
The new gating formula `(1 + learnable_scale * binary_spike)` is simpler while retaining the ability to learn the strength and polarity (boost or inhibit) of the modulation through the `learnable_scale` parameter. If `learnable_scale` is positive, spikes boost the output; if negative, they inhibit it. This allows for adaptive computational mechanisms based on spike activity.

**4. Sensible Default Initialization for Learnable Scales:**
The `attn_gate_scale` and `mlp_gate_scale` parameters are now initialized to `torch.zeros(1, 1, d_model)`. This ensures that, by default, the gating mechanism introduces no initial modulation (since `1 + 0 * binary_spike = 1`). This provides a clean baseline for training, allowing the network to learn beneficial modulation strengths from scratch, which is a sensible default for new features as per requirements.

Overall, these modifications aim to improve the neuromorphic integration of the `SpikingFLABlock` by making the spike-gating mechanism more fundamental to the network's spiking dynamics, potentially leading to more efficient information processing and better performance in sequence modeling tasks by leveraging the distinct characteristics of spiking signals.
