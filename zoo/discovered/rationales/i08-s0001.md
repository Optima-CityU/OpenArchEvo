Idea:
The primary objective of this architectural evolution is to enhance the `SpikingFLABlock` with adaptive computational mechanisms, promote multi-scale information integration, and implicitly encourage sparsity/lower firing rates through learnable gating. The following key innovations have been implemented:

1.  **Removal and Integration of SpikingMLP**: The `SpikingMLP` class has been removed. Its functionality (a two-layer feed-forward network) is now directly integrated into the `SpikingFLABlock`. This change increases the cohesion of the `SpikingFLABlock` by making it a more self-contained processing unit and simplifies the overall architecture.

2.  **Adaptive Spiking Residual Gating**: Two new `SpikingLinear` layers, `self.attn_gate_proj` and `self.ffn_gate_proj`, have been introduced. These layers process the input to their respective sub-blocks (Attention and FFN) and generate a learnable, sigmoid-activated gate signal. This gate then adaptively scales the contribution of the attention and FFN outputs to their respective residual connections. This design offers several benefits:
    *   **Adaptive Computation**: The model can dynamically adjust the influence of its sub-blocks based on the input, leading to more flexible information flow and potentially reducing redundant computations. This aligns with the 'Adaptive Computational Mechanisms' objective.
    *   **Multi-Scale Information Integration**: By learning to weigh the transformed features (from Attention/FFN) against the direct input, the block can better integrate information across different processing stages and semantic scales.
    *   **Implicit Firing Rate Control**: When a gate produces a low value, it effectively down-weights the output of its corresponding sub-block. While not directly manipulating spike rates, this mechanism can reduce the overall 'impact' of high-firing activity from a sub-block if its contribution is deemed less relevant by the learned gate, implicitly leading to more focused and potentially sparser information propagation through the residual path.
    *   **Consistent Spiking Paradigm**: Both gate projections uniformly utilize `SpikingLinear`, ensuring that all internal linear layers receive binary spike signals, thus strictly adhering to the SNN requirements.

3.  **Uniform SpikingLinear Usage**: All linear transformations within the block, including the newly introduced gate projections and the integrated FFN layers, consistently employ the `SpikingLinear` wrapper. This ensures that the core principle of spike-driven linear operations is maintained throughout the evolved `SpikingFLABlock`.

These modifications result in a `SpikingFLABlock` that is more adaptive, efficient, and better aligned with neuromorphic computing principles by leveraging spiking neurons for dynamic control over information flow and enhancing the model's ability to process and integrate information.
