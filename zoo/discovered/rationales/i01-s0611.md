1.  **Enhanced Locality and Multi-Scale Integration (GatedSpikingMLP):** The original `SpikingMLP` in Version 2 was a standard two-layer MLP. By replacing it with the `GatedSpikingMLP` (adapted from Version 1 with an additional `F.silu` activation), we introduce a more powerful non-linearity and a gating mechanism (`gate_out * F.silu(up_out)`). Crucially, the `GatedSpikingMLP` re-introduces an internal `ShortConvolution` (`mlp_pre_conv_proj` and `mlp_conv`) which operates on the gated hidden representation.
